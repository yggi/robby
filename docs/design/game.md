# The game — who it is for, what it teaches, and what ships

Start here before arguing about a feature. Three of these pages are the brief
and the rules that follow from it; one is the inventory of what exists; the last
is the only evidence anybody has that any of it works.

## Where to go instead

- How a rule is *rendered* — the colours, the ribbon, the characters, the sound
  — is a feel question: [`feel.md`](feel.md).
- Whether a room is any good is not a design argument, it is
  [`code/solver.md`](code/solver.md). Ask it before writing a heuristic.
- What is unresolved about the content is `docs/NOTES.md`; what is being built is
  `docs/BOARD.md`.

## The shape of it

Every page here is downstream of one sentence: **the player cannot read.** The
audience page states it, the thesis page says what is being taught instead of
text, the rules page is the smallest mechanic that teaches it, and the content
page is what has been built on that mechanic so far.

The playtest page is marked exploratory and stays that way. It is one child, and
she is the child the game is dedicated to, which makes it the strongest evidence
available and the least generalisable at the same time. Treat what it says about
*how* she solved things as data and what it says about children as a hypothesis.
